---
name: Hydroponics dosing
description: Safety and accuracy constraints for hydroponic nutrient and pH recommendations.
---

Hydroponic advice must separate published reference ranges from measurements of the grower's own system. Never present generic fertilizer grams, water consumption, timer cycles, or plant capacity as exact. Nutrient quantities may only be scaled from a user-entered product-label dose and its reference water volume; CE cannot identify individual nutrient deficiencies, and pH-corrector quantities cannot be calculated from pH alone because alkalinity and product strength matter. University Extension ranges are not a local laboratory validation for every Mozambican cultivar, water source, or product.

**Why:** Commercial fertilizer composition, water chemistry, spacing, installation dimensions, and local growing conditions vary; fabricated precision can lead to crop damage or wasted inputs.

**How to apply:** Require actual system dimensions and measurements before calculating. Identify the source beside any published range, describe geometric plant counts as estimates, and never infer a nutrient recipe from CE. Scale a label dose only when both the dose and label volume are supplied; tell growers to verify product suitability, add gradually, mix, and remeasure pH/CE. Do not estimate daily water use or substrate cycles without system measurements.
