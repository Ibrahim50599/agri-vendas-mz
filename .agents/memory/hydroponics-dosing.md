---
name: Hydroponics dosing
description: Safety and accuracy constraints for hydroponic nutrient and pH recommendations.
---

Hydroponic nutrient quantities are starting estimates for pure salts, not universal prescriptions for commercial fertilizer products. pH-corrector quantities cannot be calculated from pH alone because water alkalinity and product strength matter.

**Why:** Commercial fertilizer composition and source-water chemistry vary, so presenting a fixed formula as exact for every grower could damage crops.

**How to apply:** Keep these limits visible anywhere the app calculates nutrient or pH corrections. Tell growers to follow product labels, add gradually, mix, and remeasure pH/EC before adding more.
